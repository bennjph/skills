# Deck Brief Template

Use this before generating, revising, or maintaining a presentation.

The goal is to define the deck job clearly enough that the model does not invent the strategy and the slides at the same time.

## Minimum completeness rule

Do not start deck generation until these are clear enough:

- audience
- decision or outcome needed
- controlling idea
- target density or slide-count pressure
- required evidence

If those are missing, the model should pause and surface the gap instead of filling it with generic deck structure.
